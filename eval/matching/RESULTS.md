# Matching eval (v8)

Generated by `npm run eval:matching` (scripts/eval/matching/run.ts). TREC Clinical Trials cancer subset, human qrels (0 not relevant, 1 excluded, 2 eligible), structured answers per topic. Dev = TREC 2021 (10 topics), test = TREC 2022 (2 topics) + TREC 2023 (5 breast-cancer template topics, which give no age or sex: age 50, female assumed). Means over topics with bootstrap 95% CIs (4000 resamples over topics). Not a clinical-accuracy claim.

Arms: **baseline** = v5 pipeline order; **resolver** = the top 15 of that order (what rankTrials returns and find-flow reorders) re-ranked by deterministic per-criterion labels (Tier A first, then incScore), rest unchanged; **llm** = resolver + Groq judge on the unknown items of the top 5 (MATCH_JUDGE.topN; screening-only items are not sent); **resolver, full pool** = exploratory, the resolver tiering over every candidate (not what the app does).

Scope: 10 dev topics and 7 test topics. The candidate pool is each topic's judged trials (the TREC pool), not the national index. Groq free tier: requests are capped at 16000 chars (see LLM judge notes).

Definitions: P@10 = share of the top 10 judged eligible (2). Missed exclusions = share of the top 10 that TREC judged excluded (1) and the arm still shows in Tier A (baseline has no tiers, so all shown count). Unknown share = unknown labels / all criterion labels of the top-10 trials.
