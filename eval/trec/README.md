# TREC Clinical Trials (2021, 2022): external, human-labelled retrieval benchmark

An external check on TrialBridge's matching, independent of our own vignettes and labels. The TREC Clinical Trials
track (NIST/trec-cds.org) gave participants synthetic patient descriptions ("topics") and had medically trained
assessors judge ClinicalTrials.gov trials for each one: 0 = not relevant, 1 = the patient is excluded by the
criteria, 2 = the patient is eligible. Both years together have 125 topics across all of medicine; this benchmark
keeps only the topics whose patient has a malignancy, since that is what TrialBridge covers.

Terms of use and download provenance (URLs, checksums, licence conclusion): [TERMS.md](TERMS.md).

## Files

| File | What |
| --- | --- |
| `topics-cancer.json` | The cancer topics: `[{ year, topicId, text }]`, text verbatim from the TREC XML (entities decoded). Generated by `scripts/eval/trec/select-topics.ts` from a hand-curated id list. |
| `structured-answers.json` | TrialBridge questionnaire answers per topic, keyed `"<year>-<topicId>"`, using only option ids from the question catalog, plus a one-line `rationale`. |
| `TERMS.md` | Data sources and terms. |
| `data/cache/trec/` (gitignored) | `topics2021.xml`, `qrels2021.txt`, `topics2022.xml`, `qrels2022.txt`, and `studies/` (raw ClinicalTrials.gov API pages for every judged trial plus `index.json`). |
| `scripts/eval/trec/load.ts` | Loader (`loadTrec`), the placeholder-site adapter, `trecBm25Baseline`, `ndcgGraded`. |
| `scripts/eval/trec/report.ts` | Prints the counts below and the BM25 baseline. |
| `tests/unit/trec-load.test.ts` | Unit tests for nDCG (hand-computed values), the placeholder-site adapter, the parsers, and that every structured answer uses valid catalog option ids. |

## Cancer topics

All 125 topics were read individually (not just regex-matched). A topic counts as cancer when the description names
a malignancy, or describes one unambiguously (2022-6 never says "mesothelioma" but gives the pathognomonic biopsy).

Borderline cases that were excluded, and why:

- 2021-7: HCV cirrhosis with a rising AFP; hepatocellular carcinoma is suspected but not diagnosed or named.
- 2021-10: indolent systemic mastocytosis in a 22-year-old. A clonal mast-cell neoplasm, but not named or treated as a malignancy in the topic.
- 2021-20, 2021-56, 2022-37: pituitary adenoma (acromegaly, Cushing). Benign.
- 2021-67: cervical squamous intraepithelial/glandular lesion offered conization. Pre-malignant, not cancer.
- 2021-5 (colon polyps, thyroid nodule), 2021-27 and 2021-64 (biopsy negative for HCC), 2021-34 and 2021-70 (the husband has colon cancer), 2022-10 (ganglion cyst), 2022-25 (cholesteatoma), 2022-48 (lipoma): no malignancy in the patient.

Included: 2021-13 is a 62-year-old whose past medical history includes marginal zone lymphoma treated with R-CHOP in 2003; his presenting problem is urosepsis with a neurogenic bladder. The topic names a malignancy, so it is in the set, but most trials judged eligible for him are not cancer trials (see the table below).

## Notes on the data

- **2021 snapshot vs the live API.** The assessors judged the trial records as they were in the track's April 2021
  ClinicalTrials.gov snapshot. Records fetched today may have different eligibility text, status, phases or sites, and
  two judged trials are gone. A judgment can therefore disagree with the current record through no fault of the
  system. We did not download the track's multi-gigabyte XML collection.
- **Non-U.S. and non-recruiting trials.** TREC relevance ignores location and recruiting status; TrialBridge only
  ingests trials with a recruiting U.S. site. The loader (not `lib/`) gives every study with no usable U.S. site one
  synthetic location (`facility "TREC placeholder"`, Bethesda, Maryland, United States, status RECRUITING, no
  coordinates) before calling `buildRecord`, and flags the card `syntheticSite: true`. Distance filters are
  meaningless for those cards; the benchmark is for retrieval and eligibility logic, not geography.
- **Not every eligible trial is a cancer trial.** TREC judges mark a patient eligible for any trial the record
  describes, including trials for their other conditions (antibiotics for the pancreatic-cancer patient's abscess,
  dialysis trials for the myeloma patient, urology trials for 2021-13). `buildRecord` drops those as out of scope, so
  "eligible that are cards" is the ceiling for TrialBridge on each topic. Scores should be computed over the loadable
  trials (as `report.ts` does) or the gap should be reported explicitly.
- **Structured answers are author-written.** They map each topic's free text onto the
  questionnaire's option ids as carefully as possible, leave out anything the topic does not state, and record the
  reasoning in `rationale`. Two topics have internal inconsistencies in
  the source text (2021-17 names both an allogeneic and an autologous transplant; 2021-25 says right breast then left
  upper outer quadrant); the rationale says how each was resolved.
- **Topics are admission-note style, not patient voice.** TREC topics are clinician-written case summaries with
  abbreviations and lab tables. They exercise the retrieval stack, not the plain-language intake flow.
- **Small, uneven set.** 12 topics (10 from 2021, 2 from 2022) dominated by hematologic and breast cases. Treat
  per-topic numbers as diagnostics and the mean as a coarse signal, not a leaderboard figure.

## Re-downloading

```sh
mkdir -p data/cache/trec
for f in topics2021.xml qrels2021.txt topics2022.xml qrels2022.txt; do
  curl -sSL -A "Mozilla/5.0" -o "data/cache/trec/$f" "https://trec.nist.gov/data/trials/$f"
done
npx tsx scripts/eval/trec/select-topics.ts   # rewrites eval/trec/topics-cancer.json from the curated id list
npx tsx scripts/eval/trec/fetch-studies.ts   # ~40 throttled API requests; resumable; --refresh to refetch
npx tsx scripts/eval/trec/report.ts
```

NIST's server sits behind Cloudflare and rejects non-browser User-Agent strings with 403, hence the `-A`. Compare
checksums with the table in TERMS.md. The study fetch uses the same field list as ingestion (`CTGOV_FIELDS`), stays
under 45 requests per minute, sends a descriptive User-Agent and retries 429/5xx with backoff.

## v7: TREC 2023 breast-cancer topics (test split)

TREC 2023 replaced free-text cases with **templates** per disorder (40 topics, 8 disorders). The 5 "breast cancer" topics (16-20) are the only cancer ones and join TREC 2022 as the v7 test split (7 topics), held out from all v7 tuning. Their text in `topics-cancer.json` is one "field: value" line per filled template field. The templates give **no age or sex**: the structured answers assume age 50 and female for all five (stated in each rationale), and stage is left unanswered where the template does not give it. Judged trials (about 1,000 per topic) were fetched on 2026-10-19 into separate `batch-extra-*` files, so the 2021/2022 records fetched on 2026-10-09 are unchanged (`index.json` `extraFetchedAt`). Even with 2023, TREC Clinical Trials has only 17 cancer topics in total.
